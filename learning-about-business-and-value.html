Learning About Business by Looking for Real Problems

I am interested in entrepreneurship because it asks a practical question: what problem can we solve in a way people genuinely value?

By Aviral Rajput · Published 2026-10-11 · 4 minute read

Begin with people

An idea may sound impressive to its creator and still fail to help anyone. I want to understand people's daily problems, how they solve them now, and what makes an alternative genuinely useful.

Value before hype

A business is not only a name, logo, or website. It needs to create value, earn trust, and work reliably. Listening to potential users is often more informative than assuming we already know what they need.

Small experiments

A simple prototype, a conversation with a shop owner, or a test with a few users can reveal what needs to change. Experiments should be small enough that the lessons are useful even if the first version does not work.

What I hope to learn

I am still learning about business, technology, communication, and execution. I want to develop the habit of observing carefully, testing assumptions, and improving based on evidence.

This article reflects my current experience and perspective as a student. It is a learning journal, not professional advice.
