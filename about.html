About Aviral

A learner, building with intention.

I’m a student interested in personal development, technology, thoughtful business, and the process of turning knowledge into useful skills.

My story is still being written.

My mid-teen years were not always easy. There was a period when I felt disconnected from the person I wanted to become. Over time, I started reflecting on my choices and looking for healthier, more constructive ways to use my attention.

I began exploring books, exercise, studying more intentionally, and learning about web development. One important change was trying to turn more of my screen time into learning time—while also understanding that rest and balance matter.

I am not presenting myself as an expert or claiming financial independence. I am a student at an early stage, building knowledge and practical skills step by step.

What I’m exploring

Curiosity into capability.

My interests include web development, beginner programming, personal growth, books, productivity, entrepreneurship, and ways technology can solve everyday problems. I want to learn by building small projects, listening to people, and improving through feedback.
