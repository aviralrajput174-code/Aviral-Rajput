Why I Am Building a Personal Website

A personal website gives me a home for my work, ideas, learning notes, and evolving professional identity.

By Aviral Rajput · Published 2026-10-11 · 4 minute read

A space I can organise

Social platforms are useful, but a personal website lets me organise my story and writing in one place. It can include an introduction, projects, contact details, and longer articles that are easy to revisit.

A public learning journal

I want this website to show the process behind my development, not just a polished headline. Articles can record what I am learning, what I have tried, and what I still need to understand.

Clarity and credibility

A professional online presence should be accurate and consistent. That means using the same name and profile details across platforms, explaining projects honestly, and avoiding claims that cannot be supported.

A long-term asset

The website is a starting point. Over time, I can add completed projects, useful resources, and reflections that demonstrate my skills through evidence rather than promises.

This article reflects my current experience and perspective as a student. It is a learning journal, not professional advice.
