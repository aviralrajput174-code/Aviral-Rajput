Why I Started Reading Books About Growth and Thinking

Books became one way for me to slow down, examine my assumptions, and learn from ideas beyond my immediate surroundings.

By Aviral Rajput · Published 2026-10-11 · 4 minute read

Learning beyond the classroom

School gives an important foundation, but I also wanted to understand habits, focus, decision-making, business, and the way people approach difficult problems. Reading gave me a way to explore those questions at my own pace.

Ideas are only a beginning

A highlighted sentence is not the same as a changed habit. I try to ask what an idea means in everyday life and whether I can test it in a realistic way. Sometimes I apply it; sometimes I disagree or need more evidence.

Reading as a practice

I do not believe that reading alone guarantees success. It can, however, give us better questions and useful mental models. Practice, feedback, and experience are what help those ideas become skills.

What I want to share

Through this blog, I plan to share reflections on the books and concepts I explore, in my own words, with an emphasis on practical lessons rather than exaggerated claims.

This article reflects my current experience and perspective as a student. It is a learning journal, not professional advice.
